13Affiliate Program Earnings

If you participate in the Taliferro affiliate program, the following applies to your affiliate earnings. A plain-language summary is on the How affiliate earnings work page.

  • Attribution. A visitor is credited to the first affiliate link they click. That credit lasts 30 days. A referral is attributed to you only if the visitor creates a new account during those 30 days. Existing accounts, accounts you create or control for yourself, and sign-ups the Company cannot connect to your link (for example, sign-ups made only inside a mobile app) are not attributed.
  • Commission. You earn fifteen percent (15%) of the first payment on each new subscription purchased by a customer attributed to you, provided the purchase or free-trial start occurs within 30 days of the customer's first click on your link. For App Store purchases, commission is calculated on the price before Apple's fees. Except for Enterprise subscriptions, renewals and later payments do not earn commission. Separately billed implementation or customization services do not earn commission.
  • Enterprise subscriptions. For an Enterprise subscription attributed to you, you earn fifteen percent (15%) of each monthly subscription payment the Company receives, for as long as the customer continues to pay for that subscription. No commission is earned for any period the customer has not paid.
  • Refunds. If a payment that earned commission is refunded or reversed, the commission on that payment is deducted from your balance.
  • Visitor credit. You earn one cent ($0.01) for each unique visitor you refer, as determined by the Company, up to 500 visitors per day. Automated, fraudulent, or incentivized traffic does not count.
  • Starting bonus. Each affiliate is offered a one-time $100 starting bonus. It becomes part of your available balance only after you have referred 10,000 unique visitors or earned $100 in commission. Affiliates who previously received the Company's earlier $100 visitor bonus have already received this bonus.
  • Payments in other currencies. Commission on purchases made in a currency other than U.S. dollars is reviewed and credited manually.
  • Balance and adjustments. Your available balance is shown on your affiliate dashboard. The Company may correct errors and may withhold or reverse credits it reasonably believes resulted from fraud, self-referral, or a breach of these Terms and Conditions.
  • Disclosure. You must clearly disclose your affiliate relationship when promoting Taliferro, as required by the FTC Endorsement Guides and other applicable law.
  • Changes. The Company may change these program terms by updating this page. Changes apply from the date they are posted.
