Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process personal data under the following lawful bases as defined in Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Account registration, authentication, contact management, email delivery, and billing — processing necessary to provide the TODD service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): Platform security (fraud detection, rate limiting, audit logging), service improvement (aggregated analytics), and detecting abuse.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Marketing emails and product update communications. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Retaining billing records and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — Rights for California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: You may request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you, the purposes for which it is used, and any third parties it has been shared with. Submit a Privacy Rights Request or email info@taliferro.com.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account directly through the platform (Settings → Update Profile → Delete Account), or contact us at the address below.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: Taliferro Tech, LLC does not sell or share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is no action you need to take. If this practice ever changes, this policy will be updated and you will be notified.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

To exercise your rights, contact us at info@taliferro.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within 45 days.
