13 · Risks

What could slow us down

Revenue is early

2026 revenue is $38,000 invoiced to three customers, and 74% of it comes from two of them.

Sales are founder-led

We haven't yet shown a repeatable way to win customers without the founders selling.

A new category

Customers understand a single app faster than sixteen working as one, so part of the job is education.

Focus

Many apps compete for a small team's time. The raise is meant to fund that focus.

Key people

The platform depends on its lead developer. A written succession plan names a successor and is being completed.
